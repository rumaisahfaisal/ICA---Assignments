# referee's note (before ai)


the cleaned dataset shows that all four items experienced an increase between their first and last recorded dates [o]. staple a increased from 104.0 to 117.5 pkr with an overall mean of 111.17 pkr and a median of 111.75 pkr [o]. oil b had a mean of 324.83 pkr and a median of 324.00 pkr while its first-to-last increase was 15.28% [o]. pulse c showed the smallest increase of 3.69% with a mean of 205.92 pkr and a median of 206.00 pkr [o]. sweetener d had the highest percentage increase at 19.41%, with both its mean and median at 166.75 pkr [o].

one likely reason for differences between the mean and median is that unusually high or low observations can affect the mean more [e]. this can be seen in sweetener d where including the flagged value of 9999 changes the mean from 166.75 to 576.81 pkr while the median only changes from 166.75 to 167.75 pkr [o]. this suggests that the median can sometimes give a better idea of a typical price when unusual values are present [e].

however these increases do not necessarily explain what is happening in every market or why prices are changing [c]. the data only covers two cities and does not provide enough information to establish the reasons behind the price changes [c].
